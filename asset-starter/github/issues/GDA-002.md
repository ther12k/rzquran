## Objective

Produce a single consistent mascot with four expressions, using the brief. At most two optional decorative background layers are permitted. Internal MVP may proceed without these assets; adopting this task does not silently make them a blocker for GDM-026.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deliver actual isolated transparent files and available source/master files, not a flattened screen or only a prompt.
- [ ] Keep canvas, baseline and character proportions consistent; inspect alpha edges and small-size readability.
- [ ] Do not bake Arabic, instructional labels, numbers, or reward/fluency claims into art.
- [ ] Record origin, author/generation history, source terms, hashes, and genuine design-owner review.
- [ ] New learner-facing artwork follows the existing GDM-027 rights/review gate where applicable; do not fabricate approval.
